# Meeting notes

Keep a concise record that someone who missed the meeting could read in two minutes and know what happened.

## What to capture

- The topics discussed, in the order they came up, each with the few points that mattered: facts, numbers, dates, proposals, objections, and the reasoning behind them.
- Decisions, stated as what was decided, with who decided it when that is clear.
- Questions that were raised and not answered, and disagreements that were not settled.

Leave out greetings, small talk, audio troubleshooting and repetition. Do not list action items; another skill handles those.

## Format

Start with one or two sentences saying what the meeting is about. Rewrite them as the purpose becomes clearer. Then:

### Topics

A bold topic name followed by 2 to 5 bullets per topic. When the conversation returns to an earlier topic, add to that topic instead of starting a new one.

### Decisions

One bullet per decision. Omit this section while there are none.

### Open questions

One bullet per question. Remove a question once the meeting answers it, and record the answer under its topic.
